# Code of conduct

Tz Global Event Timer is maintained by one person. That person is disabled and has other life obligations. The work matters. A reply may never come, and no date is promised.

This file describes how to treat people here.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient.
- Assume good intent.
- Comment on the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time, access needs, and privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, harassment, or hate toward a person or a group.
- Sexual language or attention that is not welcome.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, scams, or repeated demands for a response.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces tied to this repository.

## How to report a problem

Open an issue when the report can be public.

When the report should stay private, contact [Matthew Craig](https://github.com/taggedzi) through GitHub. Leave private personal details out of public issues.

A report may sit unread for a long time. There may be no reply. When action is possible, the maintainer may edit, hide, or close a comment, an issue, or a pull request, or block an account.
